OS Concepts Used

  • Process: Students are treated as processes.
  • Resource: Hostel facilities are treated as resources.
  • Mutex: Only one student can use a single-instance resource.
  • Semaphore: Keeps track of available resource instances.
  • Waiting Queue: Students wait when a resource is unavailable.
  • Deadlock: Occurs when processes wait for resources held by each other.
  • Resource Allocation Graph: Shows resource requests and allocations.
